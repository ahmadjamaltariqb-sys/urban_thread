## Brand & Style
The brand embodies an understated, architectural elegance rooted in contemporary fashion retail. Inspired by the editorial restraint of COS, the sharp modernity of Zara, and the systematic clarity of Uniqlo, the visual language balances high-fashion editorial prestige with frictionless digital utility.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Refined Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Monochrome & Warm Neutrals:** High-contrast foundation structured by deep obsidian tones and warm oatmeal surfaces.
- **Architectural Structure:** Strict adherence to clean lines, intentional grid discipline, hairline dividers, and deliberate whitespace.
- **Tactile Restraint:** Zero extraneous drop shadows or skeuomorphic embellishments. Tactility is expressed through flat tonal shifts, crisp edges, and typographic tension between a high-fashion display serif and an engineered geometric sans.

## Colors
The color palette establishes an editorial canvas where photography takes precedence, anchored by crisp text and warm structural tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#111111`):** Deep Obsidian Black. Applied to primary CTAs, typography, stark hairline borders, and primary focal points.
- **Secondary (`#EAE5D9`):** Structured Oatmeal. Serves as a tactile, warm container layer for product frames, module highlights, and active toggles.
- **Tertiary (`#B91C1C`):** Editorial Crimson. A measured, high-impact accent strictly reserved for seasonal collection markers, sale micro-badges, and live availability signals.
- **Neutral (`#F4F1EA`):** Soft Canvas Beige. The default surface tone across editorial cards, side drawers, and navigation panels, softening digital glare and providing warmth over stark pure white.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure White. Used selectively to isolate garments, backdrops, and active form controls.
- **Charcoal Neutral (`#4A4A4A`):** Used for supporting editorial body text, meta specifications, and tertiary UI actions.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5E2DC`):** Muted Sand. Used across dividing lines, card perimeters, and layout separations to provide quiet structural alignment.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on an editorial dialogue between the high-contrast elegance of **Bodoni Moda** and the clean, contemporary legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headlines (Bodoni Moda):** Styled with classic proportions and high stroke contrast. Reserved for collection introductions, campaign heroes, and product names.
- **System Interface & Functional Details (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric yet humanist, calibrated for legibility at small sizes.
- **Labels & Micro-Badges:** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.16em`) to echo high-end atelier garment labeling.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a structured 12-column responsive grid that transitions into deliberate, asymmetric editorial arrangements.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 48px outer margins, 24px column gutters. Allows multi-column product matrices (2, 3, or 4 items per row) and panoramic lookbook layouts.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, 32px outer margins, 16px gutters. Converts grids to 2-up product modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, 16px outer margins, 12px gutters. Editorial products shift to full-width or staggered 2-column configurations.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Generous outer margins and vertical spacing (`space-xl` / 56px) give individual fashion pieces breathing room comparable to physical gallery catalog prints.
- Dense, technical clusters (e.g., size charts, stock indicators, and SKU metadata) rely on tight increments (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to retain utilitarian functional clarity.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy is articulated through flat spatial division, tonal contrast, and 1px structural borders rather than drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base level: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Oatmeal (`#F4F1EA`).
  - Active containers and hovering drawers: Layered using tonal transitions (`#EAE5D9`) without synthetic elevation.
- **Hairline Boundaries:**
  - All separations, grid lines, and interactive module boundaries use precise 1px solid lines tinted in Muted Sand (`#E5E2DC`) or Deep Obsidian (`#111111`) for high-priority outlines.
- **Overlays & Modals:**
  - Quick-view drawers and navigation flyouts slide along the plane backed by a 40% translucent obsidian overlay (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.4)`), preserving sharp structural edges.

## Shapes
The design system operates strictly on **Sharp (0px)** geometry. 

All interactive targets, cards, image containers, input fields, and tags adhere to precise 90-degree right angles. This architectural discipline emphasizes fabric textures and photographic compositions, evoking an uncompromising, modernist fashion aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid Deep Obsidian (`#111111`) background, Pure White text, 0px border radius, uppercase typography with tracked spacing. On hover, the style cleanly inverts: Pure White background, Obsidian text, and a crisp 1px `#111111` outline.
- **Secondary Button:** 1px border in `#111111`, transparent background, `#111111` text. On hover, fills solid with `#111111` and shifts text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost / Text Button:** Underlined with a 1px hairline offset by 4px; hover state transitions text to Charcoal (`#4A4A4A`).

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Edge-to-edge photography housed in a sharp frame. No exterior shadow.
- **Interaction:** Hovering initiates a smooth transition to an alternate editorial silhouette or model shot. An inline "Quick Add" trigger surfaces flush along the lower boundary with an inverted black bar.
- **Meta Block:** Product title set in small clean geometric sans, followed by color swatch indicators and price aligned on a single horizontal plane.

### Micro-Badges & Tags
- **New / Editorial Tag:** Minimalistic badge with 1px border in `#111111`, `#111111` text, uppercase, 0px radius.
- **Sale / Archive Tag:** Subtle Crimson (`#B91C1C`) text on an Oatmeal (`#F4F1EA`) tinted base, or solid `#B91C1C` fill with white text for marked-down hero items.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** Minimalist rectangular containers with a 1px `#E5E2DC` border, shifting to `#111111` upon focus. Zero rounded corners. Labels rest flush above in uppercase `label-md`.
- **Size Selector:** Grid of square buttons (44x44px min), bordered in `#E5E2DC`. Selected state is indicated by a solid `#111111` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Disabled or out-of-stock sizes feature a diagonal 1px slash across the square.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Uncompromising square boxes with a 1px solid stroke. Selected states feature an inner solid black square rather than rounded icons.

### Navigation & Header
- **Utility Bar & Main Navigation:** Ultra-lean hairline divider (`#E5E2DC`) pinning an architectural header with centered serif wordmark and horizontally tracked category links.
- **Bag / Cart Drawer:** Slides from the right canvas edge with a pure white surface, divided into neat modular rows via 1px dividers, concluding with an expansive, fixed-bottom checkout CTA.
