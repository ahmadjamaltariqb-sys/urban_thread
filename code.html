Architecture of the Unhurried Loom

Conceived in our Gulberg III studio, Volume 04 explores the intersection between brutalist concrete geometry and indigenous Pakistani long-staple fibers. Rather than relying on synthetic surface treatments, each piece derives its silhouette from high-density weaves—340 to 420 GSM organic cotton terry, shuttle-woven selvedge denim, and hand-spun raw silk.
